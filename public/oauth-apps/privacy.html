Privacy Policy for Personal Google API Apps

Jade Rubick Consulting, LLC · Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy covers the small Google Cloud apps that Jade Rubick runs for his own use, including "email-narrative" and a command-line client for Google Calendar and Gmail. They are personal tools, not public products. They are not offered to anyone else, and the only Google account that signs in to them is Jade's own.

What the apps access

With the account owner's consent, the apps use Google APIs to read Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, and to create or change calendar events on that same account (for example, scheduling exercise classes). They request only the scopes these tasks need.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features to the account owner: a private daily journal summarizing email and calendar activity, and calendar planning. Parts of message and event content may be sent to AI service providers (such as Anthropic) solely to classify and summarize them for the account owner. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and sharing

Data and OAuth tokens are stored on the account owner's own computer. No data is shared with anyone except the service providers named above, and only to perform the processing described here.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the account owner at any time.

Contact
